About the application

Foodservice Email Bridge (“the application”) is a privately operated tool that sends product classification review workbooks to reviewers authorised by its operator. It is not a public service and is not available for sign-up.

Authorised use

Only people invited by the operator may act as reviewers. Review workbooks and their contents are provided for the review process only and should not be forwarded or published without the operator's agreement.

Information accuracy

Workbooks are compiled from publicly available product catalogue information and automated classification. They are provided for review and may contain errors or omissions; they are not a guarantee of any product's specification, availability or price.

Availability

The application is provided as is. The operator may change, suspend or stop it at any time and does not guarantee that emails will be delivered on any particular schedule.
