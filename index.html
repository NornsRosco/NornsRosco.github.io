Foodservice Email Bridge

The email delivery component of a privately operated foodservice competitor intelligence platform.

What the platform does

The platform collects publicly available product catalogue information published by foodservice suppliers and organises it so that products can be compared and classified consistently.

What Foodservice Email Bridge does

Foodservice Email Bridge sends product classification review workbooks by email to authorised reviewers, who check and confirm how products have been classified.

It uses Google's Gmail API with a single permission, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.send, granted by the operator's own dedicated Gmail account. This permission allows the application to send email from that account. It does not allow the application to read, modify or delete any mailbox contents, and the application does not request access to any other Google account or service.

Who it is for

This is a private, internal tool. It is not offered to the public, there is no sign-up, and only reviewers authorised by the operator receive its emails.
