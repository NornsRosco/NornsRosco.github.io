Privacy Policy

Effective 17 November 2026

This policy explains what information Foodservice Email Bridge (“the application”) handles and how. The application is privately operated as part of a foodservice competitor intelligence platform (“the platform”).

Google user data

The application requests one Google OAuth scope: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.send. It is authorised only by the operator's own dedicated Gmail account.

  • What it is used for: sending product classification review workbooks, as email attachments, from that account to authorised reviewers.
  • What it is not used for: the application does not read, search, modify or delete any messages, drafts, labels, contacts or settings in the Gmail account. The granted scope does not permit it to.
  • Access credentials: the OAuth credentials issued by Google are stored privately by the operator and are used only to send these emails. They are not shared with anyone.

The application's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is not sold, not used for advertising, not used to train artificial intelligence or machine learning models, and not transferred to third parties.

Information about reviewers

To deliver review workbooks, the platform holds the email addresses of authorised reviewers. These addresses are used only to send review workbooks and related messages, and are not shared, sold or used for marketing.

Product information

The platform collects product catalogue information that suppliers have made publicly available. It does not intentionally collect personal information about members of the public.

This website

This website uses no cookies, analytics, tracking or third-party scripts, and has no forms. It is hosted by GitHub Pages; GitHub may process technical information such as visitor IP addresses to operate and secure its service, as described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Retention and removal

Information is kept only for as long as it is needed to operate the review process. Authorised reviewers may ask to be removed from the distribution list at any time using the contact address below. The Gmail authorisation can be revoked at any time from the Google Account's third-party access settings, after which the application can no longer send email.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
